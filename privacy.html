Privacy note

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Who collects your details

Kind Reset is run by the Kind Reset Team. You can reach us at hellokindreset@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Your email address, to send the reset sheet and our newsletter.
  • Your first name, only if you choose to give it, so emails can greet you by name.
  • Where you signed up (for example, this page or a blog post) and which free sheet you asked for, so we send the right follow-ups.
  • Basic email activity, such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, which our email provider may record.

Who handles it for us

Our email list is hosted by EmailOctopus, which stores your details and sends our emails on our behalf. You can read EmailOctopus’s privacy policy. We don’t sell or rent your email address, and we don’t share it with the companies we write about.

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address and basic browser details when you visit a page, to keep the site running and secure.

How long we keep your details

We keep your details while you’re subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep a record of your details marked as unsubscribed so we don’t email you again by mistake. Ask us to delete it and we will, though EmailOctopus says backup copies may take up to 90 days to clear.

Confirming and leaving

  • After you sign up, we send one email asking you to confirm. Nothing else arrives until you do.
  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Using it stops future emails.
  • To have your details deleted, or to see what we hold, email hellokindreset@gmail.com.

Links to other sites

Some of our pages and emails mention or compare other companies’ products, and some of those links are affiliate links. Each of those pages says so before the first link. Once you leave our site, that company’s own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

This site doesn’t set its own tracking or advertising cookies.
