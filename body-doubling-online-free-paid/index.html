Body Doubling Online: Free and Paid Options for ADHD Adults

By the Kind Reset Team

"I don't know where to start, and I don't even want to touch the dirty stuff. So I just sit and stare at it, completely paralyzed." That's how one person on r/adhdwomen described looking at a messy kitchen (source).

Some people in that spot notice something odd: the same task can get easier the moment someone else is in the room. Not helping. Just there. That's body doubling, and you can do it for free, online, tonight.

🧠 What body doubling is (and isn't)

Body doubling means doing a task while another person is present, in person or on a video call. They might be working on their own thing. They don't coach you, check your work or do the task for you.

It's a practical habit people in ADHD communities talk about a lot, not a medical treatment. It won't fix everything, and it doesn't work for everyone. But for the "I can't make myself start" moment, it's one of the cheapest things to try.

Why might it help? People describe a few things:

  • Starting feels less lonely. The hardest minute is the first one. Saying "I'm going to do the dishes" out loud to a real person makes that minute easier to cross.
  • Someone notices. One person on r/ADHD_Programmers put it simply: "We don't need a better planning app, we need someone to look at us in the eye in real life..." (source).
  • A clear end time. A session has a stop point, which makes the task feel finite instead of endless.

🆓 Free body doubling options

Start with the free options. You may not need to pay for anything.

1. A friend on a call

Text a friend: "Want to do a 30-minute chore call? Cameras optional. I'm doing laundry." You each do your own task. You can talk or stay quiet. End the call when the timer goes off.

Tips that make it work:

  • Pick a set time ("Tuesdays, 7 pm") so you don't have to arrange it every week.
  • Say your task out loud at the start and say what you got done at the end. That's the whole structure.
  • Keep it short. 25 to 30 minutes is plenty.

2. Someone in the same house

You don't need a call if someone's home. Ask your partner, roommate or kid to sit at the kitchen table with their phone or homework while you clear the counter. Be specific: "Can you just hang out in here for 15 minutes while I do the dishes? You don't have to help."

3. Public places

Libraries and cafés work for paperwork, emails or bills. Not much use for the bathroom sink, but good for desk tasks you've been avoiding.

4. Livestreams and "clean with me" videos

Some people put on a video of someone else cleaning or studying and work alongside it. There's no real person who knows you're there, so it's weaker than a live call, but it costs nothing and is available at 2 am.

5. Online communities with co-working rooms

Some ADHD communities run voice or video co-working rooms. For example, r/ADHD's rules mention that the subreddit has its own Discord server with an accountability section (r/ADHD rules via Rankhog, checked 2026-11-15). Check the current rules of any community before joining.

🧹 How to body double for cleaning

Cleaning is where body doubling gets tricky, because you're moving around. Here's a simple setup that pairs with our 10-minute reset:

  1. Prop your phone up where the camera sees most of the room (or skip video and use audio).
  2. Say the plan out loud: ten minutes, trash, then dishes, then one surface.
  3. Set a 10-minute timer and grab one bag for trash.
  4. Trash first. Walk the room with the bag. Anything that is clearly trash goes in. Skip anything you'd have to think about.
  5. Dishes second. Carry every cup, plate and bowl to the sink. Washing them is a job for another timer.
  6. One surface third. Pick the first table, counter or nightstand you see. Clear it.
  7. Stop when the timer rings, even mid-task. Say what you did, then tick a box on the sheet's "Done counts" row: "Tick a box after each reset. No dates, no streak."

If you want a second round, do another 10 minutes. If not, you're finished.

🔁 Make body doubling part of the restart

It's worth planning body doubling for the day after you've fallen off, when starting alone can feel hardest. Put a name on your plan in advance:

  • Who can sit with me? Write two names (or "Focusmate free session") in that box on page 2 of the reset sheet.
  • When? One recurring slot a week is enough to start.
  • What's the first task? Something tiny you can finish in 10 minutes.

🩺 A note on support

Body doubling is a practical habit, not a treatment. If getting started feels impossible most days, or you're wondering whether ADHD or something else is behind it, a doctor or licensed therapist can help you sort that out.
